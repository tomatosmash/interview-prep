# 06 · 英文面试

## 英文自我介绍（脱敏模板）

> Good morning, professors. I'm XX, from XX University, majoring in XX. My overall ranking is X out of Y, and I've passed CET-6. I've done projects in several areas: first, an AI writing platform where I built the RAG module for retrieval-augmented generation; second, I reproduced a KDD19 multivariate time-series anomaly detection algorithm and improved its F1 score from 0.11 to 0.79 through 9 controlled experiments; third, a facial-control game using MediaPipe FaceLandmarker. Besides, I won a prize in an AIGC competition. I hope to continue my study in large language models. Thank you!

## 高频问答

### Why do you want to pursue a master's degree?
- To deepen my knowledge, get systematic research training, and pursue my strong interest in machine learning and large language models.

### Can you describe one of your projects in detail?
- 用 STAR 法，选最拿手的项目（如 RAG 或异常检测复现）。

### Tell me about a challenge you faced and how you solved it.
- 讲一个真实的调试/优化经历，突出"发现问题 → 分析 → 解决"的过程。

### What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Strengths: self-driven, can turn papers into working code.
- Weakness: my theoretical depth still needs improvement, and I'm working on it.

### Why do you want to join our lab?
- 提前了解导师方向，说一句"我对您实验室的 XX 方向很感兴趣，我在 XX 项目里接触过相关技术"。
